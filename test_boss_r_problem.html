Boss1 R-Value Problem Analysis

The current code applies fillets at the 4 corners of the boss rectangle (TL, BL, TR, BR).
But in mechanical engineering, R-values go at the STEP transitions where diameters change.

Key Insight

The boss is NOT an isolated rectangle. In the front view, the boss is part of a continuous cross-section profile.
The R-value applies at the STEP (diameter transition) between:

  • Gear body edge → Boss1 edge (left side of boss1)
  • Boss1 edge → Boss2 edge (right side of boss1)

The fillet curves INTO the concave notch formed by the step, NOT around the boss rectangle corners.
